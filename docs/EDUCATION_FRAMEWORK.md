# Education Framework

The simulator is organized as a case-based ventilator training environment. Each scenario has a learning objective, physiology-driven branch, formative score, summative debrief hooks, hints, references, and recertification metadata.

## Assessment

- Pre-test and post-test scores are generated in `scenarioEngine.learning`.
- Formative assessment uses guided steps, intervention effects, branch state, and score.
- Summative assessment uses OSCE checklist, practical exam status, certificate id, and CE/CME/CEU metadata.
- Feedback includes immediate cues, delayed debrief, positive reinforcement, and corrective critical-mistake review.

## References

The reference layer covers medical textbooks, PubMed, UpToDate, Cochrane Library, NEJM cases, ATS/ESICM/KSCCM guidance, Korean/Japanese/US/European guidance, and WHO oxygen guidance.

## Learning Modes

- Clinical reasoning: SOAP, DAR, DOPES, MOVE, differential diagnosis, and problem-based exercises.
- Media: anatomical illustration, schematic diagrams, imaging prompts, procedure video slot, and audio/podcast narration.
- Encounters: virtual patient, standardized patient, OSCE checklist, case-based learning, team-based learning, flipped classroom, MOOC/LMS compatibility, microlearning, just-in-time learning, refresher practice, and recertification.
